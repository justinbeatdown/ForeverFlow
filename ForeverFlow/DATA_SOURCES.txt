FOREVERFLOW v0.5.5 BETA 4 - OFFLINE QUEST REFERENCE

Offline quest records (IDs, NPC names, reported minimum levels, quest levels,
recorded pickup coordinates) were independently compiled as factual references
from the publicly accessible zone listings:
  https://questing.wiki/wow-forever/zones/17/   (The Barrens)
  https://questing.wiki/wow-forever/zones/406/  (Stonetalon Mountains)

These zone records credit the Everything Quests/EverythingQuests project and
underlying WoW Forever community sources. Everything Quests and its Forever
location data are available under the MIT license:
  https://github.com/wheelbarrel00/EverythingQuests
  https://github.com/wheelbarrel00/EverythingQuests/blob/main/LICENSE

No code from that addon is bundled. The concise quest facts here are curated,
not a full copy of its database. For attribution and upstream license details,
see the URLs above. ForeverFlow's own code is independent.

SOURCE AND QUALITY WARNINGS:
- Quest names, levels, map starts and NPC spawns are community reports, not
  guaranteed currently live in the server or verified on your character.
- A reported minimum level is not proof all chain prerequisites are satisfied.
- Locations are pickup-area hints, never an automated movement instruction.
- Coordinates can vary with server changes or map versions.
- Data deliberately focuses on early Horde/Both-faction questing in The Barrens
  and Stonetalon; other zones use existing client-observed discovery.
